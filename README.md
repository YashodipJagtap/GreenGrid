## Introduction

Green Grid is a comprehensive digital platform dedicated to tackling the global e-waste crisis. We bridge the gap between consumers and responsible recycling, making it easier for individuals and businesses to contribute to a sustainable future. Our platform combines a facility locator, educational resources, a rewarding incentive system, and an AI assistant to create a holistic solution for electronic waste disposal.

Did you know? A single person generates approximately 7.6kg of e-waste, yet only 17.4% of e-waste worldwide is properly recycled.

## Problem Statement

The rapid pace of technological advancement has led to an alarming surge in electronic waste, creating critical environmental and social challenges:

- 57.4 Million Tons of e-waste generated worldwide.
- Awareness Gap: Low public knowledge about the severe hazards of improper e-waste disposal (e.g., toxins like lead, mercury, and cadmium leaching into soil and water).
- Inaccessibility: Significant difficulty for users in finding certified and convenient e-waste recycling facilities (E-Dumpers).
- Low Participation: A lack of motivation and tangible incentives for individuals to act responsibly.
- Environmental Damage: Pollution of ecosystems and loss of valuable resources like gold, silver, and copper that could be recovered.

## Our Solution

Green Grid provides a user-friendly, one-stop platform that empowers everyone to be part of the solution. We simplify the entire process of responsible e-waste disposal through:

1. E-Dumper Locator: An interactive map to find the nearest certified recycling centers.
2. Green Rewards: A points-based incentive system to reward users for recycling.
3. Educational Hub (Insights): Curated blogs and articles to raise awareness about e-waste, green gadgets, and sustainability.
4. Eco AI Assistant: An AI-powered guide to answer questions about e-waste and recycling.
5. Convenient Pickup Services: Scheduled pickups for those who cannot reach a facility.

## Key Features

### E-Dumper Locator
- Find the nearest e-waste disposal facilities (E-Dumpers) by entering your location.
- View real-time status (Available, Full), accepted materials, operating hours, and contact information.
- Total E-Dumpers: 12 (and growing).

### Insights & Awareness
- Informative blogs and articles covering crucial topics:
  - Reduce Laptops Carbon Footprint Through IT Asset Disposal
  - Green Gadgets: Exploring Eco-Friendly Electronics Options
  - Understanding e-waste: Types and consequences
  - How to Properly Recycle Smartphones, Corporate Responsibility, and more.

### Green Rewards System
- Earn points for responsibly disposing of your e-waste.
- Redeem points for eco-friendly merchandise and certificates:
  - Certificate (1 Point)
  - Metal Badge (5 Points)
  - T-Shirt (8 Points)
  - Cap (12 Points)
  - Water Bottle, Diary, Cup, and more!

### Eco AI Assistant
- Get instant, expert guidance on e-waste management and recycling.
- Ask about disposal methods, benefits, or finding local centers.

### Scheduled Pickup Services
- Can't drop off your e-waste? We offer convenient scheduled pickups across multiple city zones (North, South, East, West, Central).

### Recycling Impact Calculator
- Visualize the positive environmental impact of recycling your devices.
- Calculate savings in energy, water, and CO2 emissions for smartphones, laptops, monitors, tablets, and printers.

## How It Works

1. STEP 1: Search. Enter your location on the website to find the nearest E-Waste Disposal Center.
2. STEP 2: Select. Browse and choose the recycling center most convenient for you.
3. STEP 3: Dispose. Visit the center and responsibly dispose of your e-waste. (Follow the center's guidelines).
4. STEP 4: Earn. Receive points based on the type and quantity of e-waste recycled.
5. FINISH: Redeem. Redeem your points for valuable rewards and a certificate of contribution.

## Installation

To run the Green Grid platform locally for development:

1. Clone the repository:
```
git clone https://github.com/YashodipJagtap/GreenGrid.git
```

2. Navigate to the project directory:
```
cd Green-Grid
```

3. Install all required dependencies:
```
npm install
```

4. Start the development server:
```
npm run dev
```

## Usage

1. Find a Center: Use the E-Dumpers page to locate your nearest recycling facility.
2. Learn: Visit the Insights section to read blogs and understand e-waste.
3. Calculate Your Impact: Use the calculator on the E-Dumpers page to see how much you can help.
4. Recycle and Earn: Dispose of your e-waste at a partner center and earn points.
5. Get Rewarded: Go to the Green Rewards page to redeem your points for gifts.
6. Get Help: Use the Eco AI assistant on the website for instant guidance.
